# Privacy Policy for REPS - AI Pushup Counter

**Last updated:** October 27, 2026  
**Application:** REPS - AI Pushup Counter (`com.axxio.reps`)  
**Developer Contact:** alic.merlivat@gmail.com

---

## 1. Overview
Welcome to **REPS - AI Pushup Counter** ("REPS", "we", "us", or "our"). We are committed to protecting your personal privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines what information we collect, how it is used, and how your privacy is protected when you use our mobile application and associated services.

---

## 2. Camera Data & On-Device Processing
- **Real-Time On-Device Pose Estimation:** REPS uses your device's camera and computer vision (MediaPipe) solely to detect pushup form and count repetitions in real time.
- **No Video Recording or Transmission:** **100% of camera frame processing happens locally on your device.** No video recordings, raw camera frames, photographs, or biometric facial identification data are ever saved to local storage, transmitted over the internet, or uploaded to our servers or third parties.
- The camera is only active while an active workout tracking session is running.

---

## 3. Information We Collect and How It Is Used
When you optionally choose to sign in to sync your progress across devices:
- **Authentication Data:** If you sign in using Google Sign-In, we collect your basic profile details provided by Google (name, email address, profile picture).
- **Workout & Fitness Statistics:** We save your workout counts (daily reps, total pushups, streak counters, and session timestamps) in a secure cloud database (Google Firebase) to provide:
  - Global and regional leaderboards / scoreboards
  - Workout history graphs and analytics
  - Friend squad challenges

---

## 4. Third-Party Services
We partner with trusted third-party providers that adhere to strict privacy and security standards:
- **Google Play Services / Firebase:** Provides authentication and encrypted cloud database storage.
- **Google AdMob:** May display non-intrusive advertisements. AdMob may collect anonymous device identifiers in accordance with Google's Advertising Policies.
- **In-App Purchases:** Subscriptions and premium unlocks (REPS PRO) are processed securely through Google Play In-App Billing. We never collect or store your credit card or financial information.

---

## 5. Data Retention and Security
- All network communications between the app and our database use industry-standard HTTPS / TLS encryption.
- We retain your workout counts as long as your account is active.

---

## 6. User Rights & Data Deletion
You retain complete control over your data:
- You can request the complete deletion of your account and all associated workout records at any time.
- To request deletion of your account and personal data, email us directly at **alic.merlivat@gmail.com** with the subject line *"Data Deletion Request"*. Your data will be deleted within 30 days.

---

## 7. Children's Privacy
REPS is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
